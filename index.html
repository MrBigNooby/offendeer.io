Overview

        
A recreation of Cologne Cathedral, the Kölner Dom, built to be walked around and looked up at. Twin spires, flying buttresses and stone tracery, set in a square of colourful old-town houses.

        
The scale stays close to the real building, so the spires feel as tall as they should when you stand at the west doors.

        
What I built

        
    
          
  • 01The west front and twin spires, with open tracery you can see the sky through.
    • 
          
  • 02Flying buttresses, pinnacles and the long nave running down the side.
    • 
          
  • 03A square of old-town houses to give the cathedral something to tower over.
    • 
          
  • 04Day and night lighting, with the spires lit gold under a full moon.
    • 
        

        
What's next

        
The interior: the nave, the stained glass and the shrine.

EREBUS.

Overview

        
EREBUS puts you in a research facility orbiting a black hole. The station is built for watching it, and the game is about how close you dare to get before it pulls you in.

        
It started as a test of how far Roblox lighting, particles and a bit of math could go. The black hole is the only real light in the scene, and getting too close ends the way physics says it should: spaghettified.

        
What I built

        
    
          
  • 01An accretion disk made from layered beams and particle rings that spin faster near the center.
    • 
          
  • 02A shadow core with a thin bright photon ring, so it reads as missing space instead of a dark sphere.
    • 
          
  • 03A pull that ramps up as you approach, ending in a scripted spaghettification sequence.
    • 
          
  • 04The research facility itself, built and lit around the view of the black hole.
    • 
        

        
What's next

        
More of the facility, research objectives, and a leaderboard for whoever gets closest and survives.

Overview

        
Hold the Colours is a Napoleonic line-battle game. France and Austria meet on open fields with line infantry, cavalry and artillery, and every regiment fights to keep its colours flying.

        
Command your line, wait for the order, then present and fire. Volleys, charges and cannon fire decide who is still standing around the flag.

        
What I built

        
    
          
  • 01Three arms of service: line infantry, cavalry and artillery, each with its own job on the field.
    • 
          
  • 02Volley fire with the drill of the period: make ready, present, fire.
    • 
          
  • 03Officer commands so one player can move and fire a whole line at once.
    • 
          
  • 04Cavalry charges with real momentum, and artillery that tears holes in a formation.
    • 
          
  • 05Uniforms, muskets and colours for both France and Austria.
    • 
          
  • 06Open fields and tree lines laid out for line battles.
    • 
        

        
What's next

        
More nations, bigger battles, and a campaign map to fight across.

Overview

        
Kombinat is a Soviet-themed factory game. Start with a patch of ground and a hammer, build production lines, automate them, and hit the quotas of the five-year plan.

        
The end goal is the space programme: everything the factory makes feeds into a rocket on the launch pad.

        
What I built

        
    
          
  • 01Placement tools for machines, conveyors and storage.
    • 
          
  • 02Production chains that turn raw materials into parts, with automation once you've earned it.
    • 
          
  • 03Five-year plans: quotas and deadlines that drive progression.
    • 
          
  • 04A space programme finale, with a rocket built from what the factory produces.
    • 
          
  • 05Propaganda-poster art direction across the icon, thumbnails and UI.
    • 
        

        
What's next

        
More machines, shared factories in multiplayer, and a race to orbit.
